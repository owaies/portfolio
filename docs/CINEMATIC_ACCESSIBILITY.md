# Cinematic accessibility review

The portfolio's cinematic experiences use video, 3D scenes, motion, and scroll-linked effects.

## Essential content
Project names, role information, navigation, and contact actions must remain available without relying on motion.

## Reduced motion
Respect the user's reduced-motion preference when choosing animation intensity or timeline behavior.

## Video
Keep video decorative when it carries no unique information. Provide readable overlaid text independently of the footage.

## Mobile
Verify that cinematic sections remain navigable on touch devices and do not trap scrolling.
